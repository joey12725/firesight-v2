CAREER MODEL
- real salary = anchor x curve(age) x exp(z - Var/2 + e'); curve is 1 at owner's age at profile.start, looked up from a monthly table. Flat{real_raise} compounds forever; Lifecycle uses the CGM cubic held flat outside ages 20-65, times (1+extra)^years. Shocks redrawn each January (not on step 0): rho 0.97, sigma low/medium/high = Low/Typical/High.
- Layoff: monthly hazard = bachelors base 0.005 x age band (2.0/1.3/1.0/0.9) x security (0.4/1/2) x 1.6 in a recession month. Spell lognormal(median 2.2 or 4.0 in the slack regime = recession or within 12 months after, x0.85/1.0/1.4 by age, sigma 1) clipped to [0.25, 36] months, fractional (partial month paid pro rata). UI: min($600 x price_level, 50% of weekly base), 1-week wait, 26 weeks (+13 in slack regime). Scar at rehire: z -= N(0.08 or 0.15, x1.5 at 50+, +0.01/month beyond 6 capped 0.10; sd 0.15). Unvested equity forfeited at layoff/end.
- Bonus: in bonus.month, target x real salary x price_level x unit-mean lognormal (cv = volatility) with 0.5 correlation to the standardised trailing-12m equity log return (mean/sd from data.history.equity_us). Scaled by share of month paid; no proration for earlier unemployment.
- Commission: expected_annual/12 x price_level x monthly iid lognormal with cv = volatility x sqrt(12), so the ANNUAL total has the entered cv (fixes v1). CPI-indexed only.
- Equity: all grants of a job use the company of equity[0]. Shares are units worth $1 today at start; `state.stock_price` is nominal relative to start. Public: price x= max(1+beta x r_mkt, 0) x unit-mean lognormal, idio var = vol^2 - (beta x sigma_mkt)^2. Vesting: even monthly, cliff pays the accrued lump (48k/48m/12m cliff = 0 x11, 12,000, then 1,000). Refresh: every 12 months worked, refresh_value x price_level / price shares over 48 months, no cliff; refresh options struck at the money. Options exercised at vest: shares x max(price - strike, 0). Private: paper value constant in real terms; monthly hazard 1-(1-h)^(1/12), none in recession months; P(fail)=fail_prob else class modest/good/large (mix interpolated across the three research stages by fail_prob), multiple log-uniform in [0.1,1]/[1,4]/[4,20]; vested shares pay then; afterwards default public dynamics (vol 0.35, beta 1.2).
- Self-employment: net_annual/12 x (1+g)^years x price_level x F; F redrawn each January, AR(1) persistence 0.5 (judgement constant), stationary cv = volatility, half-correlated with the market signal. "Recession sensitivity" is implemented through that market co-movement, not the recession flag.
- Risk switches: career_risk=false kills wage shocks, layoffs, bonus/commission/SE noise. risk.level=None kills wage shocks and layoffs only (bonus/commission keep their own volatility) - this deviates from the stub's "every shock zero". Stock prices always simulated.
- Effect of job loss on the mean (measured, 100k paths, Typical risk, age 30, no recessions): Gaussian shocks alone keep the mean on the curve within 0.5%; with layoffs mean base salary is 0.975 / 0.960 / 0.935 of the curve at 5 / 10 / 20 years, 4.2 months unemployed per 20 years, pay received 94.3% of deterministic. expected_real_salary is therefore optimistic for risky jobs.

HOUSING API AND MODEL
- Slots as in the stub; `step(state, home_national, price_level, rng)` every month for every slot, owned or not. Order in step: costs on start-of-month value, mortgage payment, rental, then appreciation. Call `buy` before that month's step (first payment and costs fall in the purchase month).
- Value: x (1+home_national) x (1+appreciation_vs_national)^(1/12) x unit-mean lognormal. Research values (local 4%/yr random walk, 10% one-off sale shock) apply at the schema default idiosyncratic_volatility 0.08 and scale proportionally with the property's setting. Sale price = value x sale shock (uses the latest step's stashed normal).
- Mortgage: interest = balance x rate/12; exact final payment; payment below interest pays interest only; `PropertyState::prepay(amount) -> applied` for extra principal; mortgage becomes None when repaid. 300k/6%/360: payment 1,798.65, balances 299,701.35 / 296,315.96 / 251,057.17 at months 1/12/120, total interest 347,514.57.
- PMI: fixed premium from the rate card's 740-759 column (0.20/0.38/0.53/0.70% by LTV band) on the original loan; removed permanently in the first month balance <= 78% of original value OR <= 80% of current value.
- Costs: property tax = rate (property's or default_tax_rate) x value assessed every 12 months from start/purchase; insurance, maintenance on current value; HOA x price_level.
- `buy(state, t, home_index, market_rate) -> Purchase`: home_index is the CUMULATIVE national index (1.0 at start); price = event price x index; basis = price + closing; no-op if owned or slot has no purchase terms; term_years 0 = cash purchase.
- `sell(state, t, selling_cost_fraction, exclusion) -> Sale`: net_proceeds may be negative; recapture = min(depreciation, gain); exclusion only for Primary owned >= 24 months (properties owned at start qualify); home/vacation losses give taxable_gain 0, rental losses negative.
- Rentals: rent = monthly_rent x real growth since profile.start x price_level; vacancy is a Markov chain of whole vacant months with the configured mean (starts let); management on collected rent; depreciation basis x (1-land)/330 per month, honouring years_in_service. `PropertyState::close_tax_year() -> f64` once a year after December: returns non-negative taxable rental income, carries losses forward per property (no $25k allowance); `Sale::passive_loss_released` is an ordinary deduction in the sale year.
- `deductible_mortgage_interest` = interest for Primary/Vacation, 0 for rentals; the acquisition-debt cap is left to tax.
